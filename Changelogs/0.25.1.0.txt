Hotfix 0.25.1.0

Additions:
1. Achievements! These were made by our wonderful community in an achievement contest a... while ago. BUT NOW THEY'RE HERE!!!
2. Some new QoL items, including items that can relocate wormholes, an improved Anomaly Locator, and eggs that change your Vision Number
3. New armor set dropped by the Excavator, focused on void replenishment
4. Jumbo Eggs and Jumbo Capsules

Fixes: 
1. Gula Vault should no longer be locked when placed by player
2. Tried fixing an issue related to dragon lens NPC spawning crashing the game in multiplayer
3. Fixed issue where bonus critical strike damage was only being applied to melee strikes from items like the Elemental Amulet and Harvester's Scythe
4. Small adjustments to worldgen code that may fix some worldgen issues related to the Abandoned Village
5. Some optimizations for Blade Generator and upgrades that should reduce lag
6. Fix: Bulwark of the ancients grants stoned immunity now, like the ankh shield
7. Fix: King Blade's tooltip now shows correct damage multiplier number (100% -> 80%)
8. Fix: Helios now only stacks up to 5 stacks of scorch, as described in the tooltip

Misc:
1. The SOTS Texture-Pack popup no longer appears
2. Hydrokinetic Antennae and Pog Shark can be exchanged into each other at a wormhole
3. Hydrokinetic Antennae's connection visual can now be hidden when vanity is disabled
4. A hidden tooltip for Essence of Emptiness appears at 15 UVN instead of 8 UVN
5. Adjust some tooltips
